# The 3-Month Plan (12 Weeks)

**How to use this:** Each week has a goal for reading, writing, and math, and one "play activity of the week."
Do 4–5 days a week. If a week's goal isn't solid, **repeat the week**. Nothing here is a race; the order is what matters.

Codes: **PMAH** = *Preschool Math at Home* (roughly one activity a day, in book order; the plan below tells you the *ideas* to be sure to hit). **HWT** = *Handwriting Without Tears: Get Set for School*. **AB** = `04-activity-bank.md`.

Daily structure (see `02-how-to-teach.md`): sounds/reading 10–15 min → move → hands/writing 10 min → math 10 min → read-aloud 15–20 min.

---

## Month 1 — Sounds and ears (Weeks 1–4)

**Goal for the month:** she says the *sound* for most lowercase letters, and she can hear "s-u-n" and say "sun."

### Week 1 — s, a, t, p (+ pre-writing lines)

| | Goal | How |
|---|---|---|
| **Reading** | Sounds for **s, a, t, p**. Pure sounds, no "uh". | One new letter a day. Give each a hand action (s = snake hand, a = "a-a-apple" biting, t = tapping, p = popping lips). 2 min review of learned sounds each day. **Oral blending** starts now: 2-sound words in robot talk ("u-p", "a-t", "m-e"). |
| **Writing** | Hand strength + vertical and horizontal lines. | Playdough squeezing, tweezers picking pom-poms, chalk lines on the driveway. HWT: the first pre-writing/stroke pages, crayon only. |
| **Math** | Count objects to 5 with one-to-one touch; subitize 1–3. | PMAH counting activities. Snack counting: touch each cracker. Dot cards 1–3: "how many, don't count, just look!" |
| **Play of the week** | **Sound treasure hunt** (AB #3): hide objects starting with /s/ around the room. |

### Week 2 — i, n, m, d (+ first words by ear)

| | Goal | How |
|---|---|---|
| **Reading** | Sounds for **i, n, m, d**. Review s, a, t, p. | She now has enough for *sat, pat, tap, tin, pin, nap, sip, mat, dad, sad*. Don't ask her to read them yet; **you** build "sat" with magnets, say it stretched "sssaaat," and let her watch the magic. Oral blending: 3-sound stretchy words (m-a-n, s-u-n, f-i-n). |
| **Writing** | Circles and crosses; grip check. | Salt tray circles, crayon "rainbow circles," HWT stroke pages. Short crayons (snap them!) to encourage finger grip. |
| **Math** | Count to 10 with objects; cardinality ("so how many?"). | PMAH. Line up 10 toy animals and count them, then say "so there are ___." Count backward from 5 while jumping. |
| **Play of the week** | **Robot dinner** (AB #6): everything at one meal is asked for in robot talk. |

### Week 3 — g, o, c, k (+ first capitals)

| | Goal | How |
|---|---|---|
| **Reading** | Sounds for **g, o, c, k** (c and k both say /k/; keep it simple). | Sound review is now 12 letters: make it a game (AB #1–5). Oral blending daily; add **segmenting**: "say *dog* like a robot." First-sound I-Spy. |
| **Writing** | First capitals: **L, F, E** (all straight lines). | Build with popsicle sticks → chalk big → wet-dry-try → one HWT row. 2–3 letters this week is plenty. |
| **Math** | Subitize to 4; compare "more / fewer / same." | PMAH comparing activities. Two piles of grapes: "which has more?" Roll a die, grab that many blocks, "don't count, just look" for 1–4. |
| **Play of the week** | **Letter-sound hopscotch** (AB #4): chalk letters in squares, jump and say the sound. |

### Week 4 — e, u, r, h, b (+ check-in)

| | Goal | How |
|---|---|---|
| **Reading** | Sounds for **e, u, r, h, b**. All five short vowels now known. | Vowel song / vowel hand signs. Keep oral blending going. **Check-in:** flash all learned letters; any she doesn't know go on the "practice pile" for daily 2-minute review. If she hears "s-u-n" → "sun" easily, she's ready for Month 2. If not, spend Week 5 repeating the oral games. |
| **Writing** | Capitals **H, T, I**. | Same 4-move routine. Start writing **A** for her name if she's ready (two big lines, one little line). |
| **Math** | Numerals 1–5 recognition; count to 15 rote. | PMAH. Numeral cards 1–5 matched to dot cards / fingers. Number hunt: find a "3" on a clock, remote, book page. |
| **Play of the week** | **Sound sorting bins** (AB #7): toys sorted into bins by first sound. |

**End of Month 1 checklist** (see `05-progress-tracker.md`): knows ~20 sounds, hears 3-sound words, draws lines/circles/crosses, counts 10 objects, subitizes 3–4, compares more/fewer.

## Month 2 — Blending real words (Weeks 5–8)

**Goal for the month:** she can sound out and blend short-vowel CVC words with a little help, and read her first Bob Book.

### Week 5 — f, l, j, v + blending "at" and "an" words

| | Goal | How |
|---|---|---|
| **Reading** | Sounds for **f, l, j, v**. **First printed blending:** *at* family. | Cookie sheet, magnets: put **-at** on the sheet. Add **s** → "sssaaat, sat!" Swap the first letter: *mat, hat, cat, pat, bat, rat*. She does the sliding finger and stretching. 3–5 words a day. Then *-an*: *man, can, pan, fan, ran, tan*. |
| **Writing** | Capitals **U, C, O**. | Circles are ready now. Bath crayons on the tub wall. HWT rows. |
| **Math** | Number bonds of 3 and 4 with fingers; "one more." | PMAH. "Show me 3 fingers. Hide 1. How many now?" Snack: "You have 2. I give you 1 more. Now?" |
| **Play of the week** | **Word family flip book** (AB #12): staple cards so the first letter flips: *_at*. |

### Week 6 — w, x, y, z, q + more short-a families

| | Goal | How |
|---|---|---|
| **Reading** | Last sounds **w, x, y, z, q** (qu = "kw"). All 26 done. Blend **-ap, -am, -ad, -ag** words: *map, cap, tap, jam, ham, dad, sad, bag, rag, wag*. | Mix families now: *cat, map, sad, jam* in one session. If she's stuck, go back to one family. Start **reading to a stuffed animal**: she reads the 3 words she built to Teddy. |
| **Writing** | Capitals **Q, G, S**. Practice her name. | S is tricky: do it in the salt tray many times before crayon. Name-writing: model it, she traces, then copies. |
| **Math** | Subitize to 5; numerals 6–10 recognition. | PMAH. Five-frame with pom-poms: "how many? how many empty?" Numeral cards 6–10 with dot cards. Dominoes: match dot sides. |
| **Play of the week** | **Blending ramp** (AB #13): toy car rolls down a ramp past three letters as she blends faster and faster. |

### Week 7 — short i and o words + Bob Book 1

| | Goal | How |
|---|---|---|
| **Reading** | **-it, -in, -ip, -ig** (*sit, hit, pin, tin, win, dip, hip, pig, dig, big*) and **-ot, -op, -og** (*hot, pot, not, top, hop, mop, dog, log, fog*). **Bob Books Set 1, Book 1: "Mat"** at the end of the week. | Mix a and i words: *cat/sit, map/pig*. Point out the vowel: "which letter is in the middle? what does it say?" Read Book 1 together: her finger under each word, you help with any word, then **reread it** the next day. Big celebration. |
| **Writing** | Capitals **J, D, P**. | Frog-jump letters: "start at the top, jump back to the top." |
| **Math** | Counting to 20; shapes (circle, square, triangle, rectangle). | PMAH. Shape hunt in the house. Build shapes with popsicle sticks; how many sides? Count steps to 20 while walking. |
| **Play of the week** | **Secret word treasure hunt** (AB #15): each clue is one CVC word she reads to find the next clue. |

### Week 8 — short u and e words + heart words

| | Goal | How |
|---|---|---|
| **Reading** | **-ut, -un, -ug, -up** (*cut, nut, sun, run, fun, bug, hug, mug, cup, pup*) then **-et, -ed, -en** (*pet, net, wet, jet, bed, red, fed, hen, pen, ten*). First **heart words: "the", "a"**. Bob Books 2–3. | Short-e is hardest to hear; do "egg" hand sign each time. Introduce "the" and "a": "We just know this one by heart." Then build tiny sentences on the cookie sheet: *the cat sat.* |
| **Writing** | Capitals **B, R, N**. Numerals **1, 2, 3**. | Numbers now join writing time. "1 is a big line down." |
| **Math** | Number bonds of 5; adding within 5 with objects. | PMAH. "5 pom-poms, hide some under the cup. How many are hiding?" Dice + blocks: roll, take that many, roll again, "how many altogether?" |
| **Play of the week** | **Sentence strips** (AB #17): silly sentences she reads and acts out: *the pig can hop.* |

**End of Month 2 checklist:** all 26 sounds; blends most CVC words with short a/i/o and some u/e; reads Bob Books 1–3 with help; writes ~15 capitals and her name; subitizes to 5; recognizes numerals to 10; adds within 5 with objects.

## Month 3 — Reading for real (Weeks 9–12)

**Goal for the month:** reads simple CVC sentences and Bob Books Set 1 with growing independence, writes all capitals and numbers 1–10, does simple adding/taking-away with objects.

### Week 9 — mixed CVC + Bob Books 4–6

| | Goal | How |
|---|---|---|
| **Reading** | Mixed CVC words with all five vowels. Heart words **I, is**. Bob Books 4–6. | Daily: 5 words on the cookie sheet (mixed vowels), then one Bob Book (new or reread). Reread yesterday's book first for speed; it's a warm-up, not a test. Sentence building: *I sit. the dog is big.* |
| **Writing** | Capitals **M, K, A**. Numerals **4, 5**. | "A" was her name letter; now formal practice. |
| **Math** | Taking away within 5 with objects; "one less." | PMAH. "5 crackers. Eat 2. How many left?" Countdown rocket with fingers: 5-4-3-2-1-blast off. |
| **Play of the week** | **Reading picnic** (AB #19): blanket, snacks, she reads one word/book to each stuffed animal. |

### Week 10 — Bob Books 7–9 + longer sentences

| | Goal | How |
|---|---|---|
| **Reading** | Bob Books 7–9. Heart words **to, and**. Read 2-sentence "stories" you write. | Write a 2-line story about her on an index card: *Ayah can hop. Ayah is fun.* She reads it, keeps it in a "my books" envelope. Progressive Phonics free books are a nice add here. |
| **Writing** | Capitals **V, W, X**. Numerals **6, 7**. | Write a whole word: *CAT* under a drawing of a cat. Labeling her drawings is a huge motivator. |
| **Math** | Patterns (AB, ABB); measuring/comparing length. | PMAH. Bead or sticker patterns: red-blue-red-blue, then red-red-blue. "Which is longer, the spoon or the fork? Let's line them up." |
| **Play of the week** | **Label the house** (AB #21): sticky notes with CVC words on objects: *bed, cup, pan, mat, dog (toy).* |

### Week 11 — Bob Books 10–12 + first "reading alone"

| | Goal | How |
|---|---|---|
| **Reading** | Finish **Bob Books Set 1**. Let her try a familiar book *alone* while you "cook." | Independence practice: she reads a book she already knows to herself or Teddy while you're nearby but not helping. Then she "teaches" you a word. If she wants, start **Set 2**. |
| **Writing** | Capitals **Y, Z**, review any wobbly ones. Numerals **8, 9, 10**. | All 26 capitals are done. Celebrate with an "alphabet poster" she writes over a few days. |
| **Math** | Adding within 5 with numerals shown ("2 + 1" with objects under it); counting to 30. | PMAH. Show "3 + 2" cards with pom-poms under each number; count all. No writing equations yet; just seeing them. Count to 30 with a hundred chart, pointing. |
| **Play of the week** | **Board game week** (AB #30): *Count Your Chickens* or *Hi Ho! Cherry-O* every day instead of a math activity. |

### Week 12 — Celebration and check-in

| | Goal | How |
|---|---|---|
| **Reading** | **No new content.** Reread favorites, make a "books I can read" list, read to a grandparent on video. | Use `05-progress-tracker.md` to see what's solid and what needs another week. |
| **Writing** | Write a card to someone: *I LOVE YOU. AYAH.* | That's the whole writing goal this week. |
| **Math** | Play every math game she loved. | Ask her to teach *you* the game. Explaining is the deepest learning. |
| **Play of the week** | **Certificate day** (AB #40): make a paper certificate, take a photo of her with her Bob Books stack. |

## What comes after Month 3

- **Reading:** consonant digraphs (sh, ch, th, ck), then Bob Books Set 2–3, then "silent e" and vowel teams. If you chose a spine (TGTB or Logic of English), just keep following it.
- **Writing:** lowercase letters (HWT *Letters and Numbers for Me*, the K level), short words and her own sentences.
- **Math:** at 5, start *Kindergarten Math with Confidence*.

## If a week goes badly

- **She's resisting:** cut everything to 5 minutes for a week, keep only the games and the read-aloud. Resistance is usually "too long," not "too hard."
- **A skill isn't sticking:** repeat the week. Or go back one step (blending trouble → oral games; letter trouble → actions and hunts).
- **She's flying:** don't add subjects. Read more books, write more labels, play harder games. Depth over speed at 4.
