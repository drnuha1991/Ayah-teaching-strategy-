# The 3-Month Plan (12 Weeks)

**Printable version:** `printables/weekly-plans.pdf` has each week below on one page with a Mon–Fri tick grid, ready to hang up.
`printables/checklists.pdf` has the hang-up checklists (letter sounds, words, books, writing, math, sticker chart).

**How the letters are organized (the *ABC See, Hear, Do* way):** letters come in **groups of 4–5**, and as soon as a group is
learned she **blends real words from just those letters**. Week 1 is A, B, C, T, M and she reads *cat, mat, bat, tab, cab* by Friday.
No waiting a month for blending. Use the book's page for each letter (picture + hand motion), and use the words printed in the book
after each group. The groups below match the book's approach; **if your copy's next group is different, follow the book** and write its
letters into the boxes on the printable.

Capitals come first (like the book's Level 1), which also lines up with handwriting. Lowercase is introduced in Week 11 and continues after the 3 months.

Codes: **PMAH** = *Preschool Math at Home* (about one activity a day, in book order). **HWT** = *Handwriting Without Tears: Get Set for School*.
**AB #** = activity number in `04-activity-bank.md`.

Daily structure: sounds & words 10–15 min → move 5 min → hands & writing 10 min → math game 10 min → story time 15–20 min. Weekends off.

**Each week, same rhythm:** Mon–Thu one new letter a day (book page, sound, hand motion, 2-minute review of all learned letters), and every day
3–5 words built with magnets on the cookie sheet (slide the finger, stretch the sounds, "c-a-t… cat!"). Fri = words only, plus a game.
Keep the ear games going all week (robot talk, "say *dog* like a robot", first-sound I-Spy).

**Week 4 check-in:** flash all 17 letters. Anything wobbly goes on the "practice pile" for 2 minutes a day. If blending is hard, do a week of only
ear games and stretchy words (*sun, man, fin*) before moving on. Repeating a week is normal.

## What comes after Month 3

- **Reading:** the rest of lowercase in the same groups, then the book's next levels (blends and digraphs: sh, ch, th, ck), Bob Books Set 2–3.
- **Writing:** lowercase letters (HWT *Letters and Numbers for Me*), short words, her own sentences.
- **Math:** at 5, *Kindergarten Math with Confidence*.

## If a week goes badly

- **She's resisting:** cut everything to 5 minutes for a week, keep only games and story time. Resistance is usually "too long," not "too hard."
- **A skill isn't sticking:** repeat the week (use the blank page at the end of the printable). Or go back one step: blending trouble → ear games; letter trouble → hand motions and hunts.
- **She's flying:** don't add subjects. More books, more labels, harder games. Depth over speed at 4.
