# The 12-Week Plan

One rule a week. Each week has **one reading page** (`printables/reading-pages.pdf`, page number = week number)
and its writing work. Repeat any week that isn't fluent by Friday; the plan is a sequence, not a calendar.

Daily: warm-up 3 min → reading page 10 min → writing 10 min → story 5 min. Weekends off.

**Codes:** RP = reading page, WP = writing page (`printables/writing-pages.pdf`), AB # = activity in `04-activity-bank.md`.

## Month 1 — Joining and long vowels

| Week | Rule | Reading (RP) | Writing (WP) | Game of the week |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1** | **Letters hold hands.** Families 1–3: ب ت ث ن ي · ج ح خ · ا د ذ ر ز و. The six that let go. | RP 1: 2- and 3-letter words with short vowels only: كَتَبَ، ذَهَبَ، جَلَسَ، لَعِبَ، هُوَ، مَعَ | WP: letter forms chart; tracing pages for families 1–3; "join the letters" page 1 | Magnet word-building (AB #1) |
| **2** | **Families 4–7:** س ش ص ض · ط ظ ع غ · ف ق ك ل م · ه ة ء لا. Middle ع and ه. | RP 2: more short-vowel words: سَمِعَ، عَمِلَ، فَهِمَ، شَرِبَ، صَنَعَ، قَفَزَ، ضَحِكَ، هَرَبَ | WP: tracing pages for families 4–7; "split the word" page; **start daily copying (4 words) + dictation (3 words)** | Body-and-tail sort (AB #4) |
| **3** | **Madd with ا and و.** Two claps. | RP 3: قَالَ، نَامَ، بَابَ، كَانَ، زَارَ، يَقُولُ، يَزُورُ، يَصُومُ، نُور | Copy + dictation. Ruled-paper page: write the madd words, circle the madd letter. | Clap the vowel (AB #8) |
| **4** | **Madd with ي** + all three mixed. | RP 4: فِي، كَبِير، صَغِير، يَبِيعُ، جَمِيل، سَعِيد؛ mixed: قَالَ، يَقُولُ، يَقِيلُ… and minimal pairs جَمَل/جَمَال | Copy + dictation. "Which madd?" sorting page. | Long-or-short cards (AB #9) |

**Week 4 check-in:** she reads RP 1–4 with tapping only on new words. If joining is still slow, repeat Weeks 1–2 with the same pages before going to sukoon.

## What comes after Week 12

- **Reading:** graded vowelled readers, 10 minutes a day; then Qur'an-style reading practice if that's a goal (the rules above are exactly the ones a Qaida course assumes); gradually text with partial tashkeel (Grade 3–4 school books).
- **Writing:** her own sentences daily (a 3-line diary in Arabic), spelling rules for hamza seats, the difference between ة/ت endings, and cursive speed.
- **Grammar** is not for now. Reading fluency and a big word bank first.

## If a week goes badly

- **Resisting:** cut to 10 minutes, one page, games only, for a week. It's usually "too long," not "too hard."
- **A rule isn't sticking:** repeat the week. Or go back one rule; a shaky sukoon is often a shaky madd underneath.
- **She's flying:** don't add rules faster. Add reading: more books, more rows, longer stories. Fluency needs miles, not new rules.
