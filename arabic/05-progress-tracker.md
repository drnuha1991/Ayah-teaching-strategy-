## If she's stuck on…

- **Joining (Week 1–2):** Do only family pages and magnet-building for a week. Reading joined words is 90% "spot the body." Play "Find the body" (AB #2) on real books.
- **Long vowels:** it's the ear. Clap games with no text for three days, then back to the page.
- **Sukoon:** check madd first; if she's stretching everything, sukoon can't land. Then "glue-it robot" daily.
- **Tanween fath alif:** dictation with "alif police" for a week.
- **Sun letters:** highlighter game. Then chant the moon sentence; everything else is sun.
- **Writing direction:** don't fight it in the reading block; fix it only in the writing block, calmly, every time. It resolves by itself around 8.
- **Speed:** rereading. Three days on the same page always works. A page she can read fast is worth more than two she can read slowly.
- **Motivation:** shorter sessions, a visible chart, and teaching Ayah. Never skip the story at the end; that is the part she'll remember loving.
