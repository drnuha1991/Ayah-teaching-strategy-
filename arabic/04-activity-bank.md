# Activity Bank — 35 Short Games

Each takes 3–8 minutes. Swap one in whenever the page feels flat. Numbers match the plan's "Game of the week."

## Joining letters and letter forms (Weeks 1–2)

1. **Magnet word-building.** Arabic magnetic letters (or letters written on bottle caps) on a tray. You say كَتَبَ, she builds it right-to-left, then reads it. Then swap one letter: كَتَبَ → كَتَمَ → رَسَمَ.
2. **Find the body.** A page of a vowelled story. "Find every ب, in any position." Highlighter. Count them.
3. **Let go!** Cards for ا د ذ ر ز و in red, all other letters in blue. She builds words; at each red card she claps once ("let go") and starts the next letter apart.
4. **Body-and-tail sort.** Cards showing letters in middle form (ـبـ ـعـ ـهـ ـكـ …). She sorts them into the 7 family piles by body.
5. **Letter dominoes.** Cards with a letter's final form on one side and another's initial form on the other; match to make joined pairs.
6. **Sand tray forms.** Write a letter's 4 forms in a tray of semolina with a finger, saying "alone, start, middle, end."
7. **Which one changed?** Two words that differ by one letter (بَابَ/نَابَ, سَمِعَ/سَمِحَ). She spots and reads both.

## Long vowels (Weeks 3–4)

8. **Clap the vowel.** You say a word; she claps once for a short vowel, twice for a long one. Then reverse.
9. **Long-or-short cards.** Pairs: جَمَل/جَمَال، عَلَم/عَالَم، كَتَبَ/كَاتَبَ، مَلِك/مَالِك. Shuffle, draw, read, say which.
10. **Stretch the elastic.** Hold a rubber band; stretch it while saying the long vowel. Physical = memorable.
11. **Madd hunt.** In a story page, circle every alif/waw/yaa that is a madd (no vowel on it). Trick: some و and ي are consonants.

## Sukoon (Week 5)

12. **Glue-it robot.** You say a word letter by letter; at the sukoon she says "glue!" and joins it to the previous letter. مِـ – نْ → "min".
13. **Sukoon cards.** Word cards with the sukoon covered by a sticky flag. She reads, lifts the flag, reads again correctly.
14. **ay/aw pairs.** بَيْت/بَات، يَوْم/يُوم(nonsense), عَيْن/عِين: which is real, which is silly? Read both.

## Tanween (Week 6)

15. **Three-cup tanween.** Three cups labelled ـٌ ـً ـٍ. Say a word ("kitaabun", "kitaaban", "kitaabin"); she drops a bead in the right cup, then writes the word with the right ending.
16. **Alif police.** Dictate tanween-fath words; she must "arrest" any missing alif with a red circle.
17. **Rhyme the ending.** She names three things in the room with tanween damm: بَابٌ، قَلَمٌ، كُرْسِيٌّ.

## Shadda (Week 7)

18. **Clap-clap shadda.** Read a word; on the shadda letter, clap twice. سَيَّارَة = sa-y-ya-ra.
19. **Double or not.** Pairs: دَرَسَ/دَرَّسَ، عَلِمَ/عَلَّمَ، كَبَرَ/كَبَّرَ. Say one; she points and reads.
20. **Shadda relay.** Write a letter twice on the board (بْبَ), then she rewrites it as بَّ. 10 letters, timed.

## ال sun and moon (Week 8)

21. **Sun & moon sort.** Word cards with ال; a sun and a moon drawn on two plates. Sort, then read each pile aloud.
22. **Highlight the silent ل.** Story page: she highlights every ل in ال that must not be said (sun letters), then reads.
23. **The moon sentence.** Learn إِبْغِ حَجَّكَ وَخَفْ عَقِيمَهُ as a chant; every letter in it is a moon letter.

## Endings and hamza (Week 9)

24. **Ending detective.** Cards ending ة / ه / ى / ي. She says the sound at a stop and when continuing.
25. **Hamza hunt.** In a page of text, circle all five hamza shapes in five colours.
26. **Girl or boy.** Nouns with and without ة: مُعَلِّم/مُعَلِّمَة، طَبِيب/طَبِيبَة. Read, sort, giggle.

## Sentences and stories (Weeks 10–12)

27. **Sentence strips.** Cut a sentence into words; she rebuilds it right-to-left and reads.
28. **Stop sign.** A stop card at the end of each sentence: she drops the last vowel there.
29. **Who did what?** After reading a sentence: مَنْ؟ مَاذَا فَعَلَ؟ أَيْنَ؟ Three questions, answered in Arabic.
30. **Story theatre.** She reads the story; you act it with toys. Then swap.
31. **Label the house.** Sticky notes: الْبَاب، الْمَطْبَخ، السَّرِير، الشُّبَّاك. She writes them with full tashkeel.
32. **Diary line.** Every evening one sentence: الْيَوْمَ أَكَلْتُ … / لَعِبْتُ … She writes; you don't correct spelling in the diary, only in dictation.

## Any week

33. **Speed row.** Time her reading a row she's already ticked. Beat yesterday's time. Chart it.
34. **Teach the little sister.** She teaches Ayah one letter or one word. Nothing cements like teaching.
35. **Certificate day.** End of month: a certificate, the "Words I can read" list read aloud to the family, and she picks the next story book.
